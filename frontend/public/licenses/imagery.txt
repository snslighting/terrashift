Built-in Earth imagery

geo/blue-marble-mercator.webp, geo/earth-day.webp
  NASA Blue Marble (NASA Earth Observatory / Visible Earth). NASA imagery is
  not copyrighted in the United States. The Mercator map version was
  reprojected from the equirectangular image for TerraShift.

geo/earth-night.webp
  NASA Earth at Night (city lights), NASA Earth Observatory / Visible Earth.

geo/earth-relief.webp
  Topography (relief) image distributed with the same examples.
